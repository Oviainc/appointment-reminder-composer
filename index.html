Reminder composer

Pick a saved appointment, choose a step, and copy a ready-to-send message in the appointment language. The sequence that works: a confirmation the moment the appointment is booked, a reminder 24 hours before, a short one on the morning of the appointment, and one on the way. When someone still does not show, send the first follow-up soon — while the slot is still fresh — and a second one two days later offering a new time. Every message we compose is logged on the appointment, so we always know what was sent and when. The appointments list suggests the next step for every booking — worked out from its time, the messages already logged, and its recorded outcome — and picking an appointment here pre-selects that step, so what to send next is decided before the moment arrives. The outcome buttons on each appointment (attended, no-show, cancelled) are part of the record too: they drive the no-show rate above, the per-client history, and which follow-up the composer suggests next. When a cancellation policy is saved for the business, it travels with the confirmation and the 24-hour reminder, under a label in the appointment language — stated in the message itself, not remembered from an earlier conversation. When the message reads right, send it straight from here by email or WhatsApp, download a calendar file (.ics) the client can add in one tap, or copy the message into any other channel.
